# PetMagix Product Overview

## Company Mission
PetMagix is a Tehran-based pet care company building a super app for pet owners through micro-SaaS solutions. Each service operates as an independent micro-startup, enabling rapid development and targeted launches.

## Current Product: FeedMagix
AI-powered pet food recommendation system that:
- Analyzes pet food labels via photo upload
- Uses multi-AI agentic system for product research and database cross-checking
- Provides simple Yes/No recommendations with scores and insights
- Compares against individual pet profiles

## Target Market
- Primary: Persian-speaking pet owners in Iran
- Secondary: Global pet owner community
- Current reach: ~6k Instagram followers (@petmagix.ir)

## Business Model
- Micro-SaaS architecture for scalable, independent services
- AI-first approach using multi-LLM systems
- Bilingual support (Persian/English)
- Cost-optimized infrastructure using free tiers

## Development Status
- FeedMagix MVP: Near completion
- Next phase: User acquisition and feedback collection
- Long-term: Expand super app with additional pet care micro-services
