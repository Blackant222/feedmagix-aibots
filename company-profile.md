# 🐾 PetMagix — Company Profile

## Overview
PetMagix is a Tehran-based pet care company with a vision to become the **go-to super app for every pet owner**.  
Our approach: **Micro-startup / SaaS-based** — each service is built as a micro-saas, allowing nimble development, targeted launches, and rapid iteration.

## Our Approach
- **Micro-service Architecture:** Independent business units operate as self-contained micro-SaaS products.
- **AI-first:** Leveraging multi-LLM systems for each service to deliver faster, more accurate results.
- **User-Centric UX:** Designed to be intuitive, fast, and enjoyable in both Persian and English.

---

## Current Flagship — FeedMagix
**FeedMagix** is our first micro-SaaS. It helps pet owners choose the right commercial pet food by:
1. Taking a photo of the pet food label.
2. Using a multi-AI agentic system to:
   - Find the product details on the internet.
   - Crosscheck with our internal pet food database.
   - Compare against the pet’s profile.
3. Giving a simple decision ("Yes" 👍 or "No" 👎) with a **score** and **insight**.
